About Moe’s Movers

Houston’s local movers who treat your home like our own.

Moe started Moe’s Movers after years of helping Houston families find their next home. Again and again, he saw how stressful it was to find movers people could trust, so he built the moving company he’d want to hire himself.

He knows Houston’s neighborhoods, and his goal is simple: a smooth, stress-free moving day with clear, upfront pricing based on the details of each move, so you can focus on the excitement of a new chapter, not the move itself.
