Questions about home moves

Here is what customers usually ask before booking a home move with us.

What is included in a home move?

Your crew, the truck, moving blankets, dollies, and furniture wrapping. We load, transport, unload, and place furniture in the rooms you choose.

Get a Free Quote
How far ahead should I book?

Two to three weeks is ideal, especially at the end of the month. If your date is closer, call us and we will do our best to fit you in.

Do you move between Texas cities?

Yes. Houston is home base, and we move families to and from cities across Texas. Those moves are quoted per move.

How is the price decided?

Your price is based on the details of your move: home size, distance, stairs, and any packing. You get it upfront, with no hidden fees.
