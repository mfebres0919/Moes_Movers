Our commitment

Moe’s Movers wants everyone to be able to use this website, including people who use screen readers, keyboard navigation, screen magnification, voice control, or other assistive technology.

We work toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA (opens in a new tab), the standard most widely used to measure website accessibility, and we review the site whenever pages are added or changed.

What we’ve done

  • Clear page structure with headings, landmarks, and a “Skip to main content” link.
  • Full keyboard support, with a visible focus outline on links, buttons, and form fields.
  • Descriptive link text, including notes when a link opens a new tab or your phone or email app.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images. Decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Labeled form fields with clear error messages on the quote and contact forms.
  • Text and controls that meet WCAG AA color contrast.
  • Animations that respect your device’s “reduce motion” setting, and moving content you can pause.
  • Regular testing with automated tools and manual checks with a keyboard and a screen reader.

Known limitations

Some parts of the site rely on outside services that we don’t fully control:

  • The service area map uses map images from Esri. Some details on the map may be hard to read with a screen reader. The same information is available in the area lists and the ZIP code checker next to the map, or you can call us.
  • Links to other websites, such as Google reviews, Facebook, Instagram, and TikTok, follow those sites’ own accessibility standards.

Need help or found a problem?

If anything on this site is hard to use, or you’d like information in a different format, please let us know. Tell us the page and what happened, and we’ll help you get a quote or answer another way, such as by phone or email. We aim to respond within 2 business days.
