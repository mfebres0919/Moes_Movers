Who we are

Houston’s local movers who treat your home like our own.

Moe’s Movers started with a front-row seat to moving day. In four years as a real estate agent, Moe helped families settle into new homes and saw firsthand what matters most during that move: a crew you can trust, showing up on time, with nothing damaged and no surprise costs.

So he built the moving company he’d want to hire himself. Today Moe’s Movers is a fully insured, licensed moving company serving Houston, its suburbs, and the entire state of Texas, combining real care for every item with clear, upfront pricing based on the details of each move.
